The Story

Two programs. One language. A year of showing up.

Since 2023, I have committed to educational volunteering in Laos — a country I had come to love, and whose language I had begun to learn.

The first program brought me to a kindergarten, where I designed and taught weekly Hangul lessons from February through December. The second sent me to Xieng Khouang province, where I served as a Korean-Lao interpreter for an education mission — twice.

This portfolio documents both. Not just what I did, but what it taught me about communication, patience, and the quiet power of showing up across cultural boundaries.

Program 2

Interpreter Mission,
Xieng Khouang

Xieng Khouang is a remote province in northern Laos — hours from the capital, with few resources and even fewer outside visitors. Since 2023, I have joined a Korean church mission team to Xieng Khouang three times (2023, 2024, 2025), serving as the sole Korean-Lao interpreter. Each trip required a full-day journey by road to reach one of Laos's most remote provinces.

Each mission was based at a local school in Xieng Khouang — known to the mission team as Somvatthana School. Over 3–7 days on-site, I interpreted between Korean team members and Lao school staff and students, enabling classroom instruction, school events, and community activities including a school painting project. The role demanded cultural sensitivity, real-time judgment, and the ability to make both sides feel genuinely heard.

Across three trips, I contributed approximately 60 hours of active service. The school issued an official verification letter in Lao — rare documentation that formally confirms this role.
