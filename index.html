The Story

Two programs. One language. A year of showing up.

Since 2022, I have committed to educational volunteering in Laos — a country I had come to love, and whose language I had begun to learn.

The first program brought me to a kindergarten, where I designed and taught weekly Hangul lessons from February through December. The second sent me to Xieng Khouang province, where I served as a Korean-Lao interpreter for an education mission — twice.

This portfolio documents both. Not just what I did, but what it taught me about communication, patience, and the quiet power of showing up across cultural boundaries.

Program 2

Interpreter Mission,
Xieng Khouang

Xieng Khouang is a remote province in northern Laos — hours from the capital, with few resources and even fewer outside visitors. Since 2022, I have joined an education mission team there three times (2022, 2023, 2024), serving as Korean-Lao interpreter.

The role required more than language. It demanded cultural sensitivity, quick thinking in real time, and the ability to make both sides feel heard. Working with local school staff, I helped bridge communication gaps that would otherwise have left the mission incomplete.

The school issued an official verification letter in Lao — documentation of service that is rare and meaningful.
